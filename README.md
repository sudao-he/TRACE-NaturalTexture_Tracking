# TRACE: Tracking Natural Textures for Visual Displacement Field Measurement Without Artificial Speckles

This repository accompanies the paper:

> **TRACE: Tracking Natural Textures for Visual Displacement Field Measurement Without Artificial Speckles**  
> Sudao He, Xuanyi Lu, Yongquan Li, and Shenghan Zhang

## Overview

Full-field displacement measurement is fundamental to structural health monitoring, experimental mechanics, and damage assessment. Conventional digital image correlation (DIC) is widely used for this purpose, but reliable measurements typically depend on carefully prepared artificial speckle patterns. This requirement can limit practical deployment on large-scale, inaccessible, in-service, high-temperature, or mechanically sensitive structures.

**TRACE** is a target-free framework for dense displacement-field estimation directly from the natural surface textures of engineering materials. The method formulates deformation measurement as a generalized dense correspondence problem, allowing the same framework to support both optical-flow-based displacement estimation and stereo-disparity-based three-dimensional reconstruction.


The framework is experimentally evaluated using stereo imaging and representative engineering materials, including **metal, timber, and concrete**, for tensile deformation, rigid-body translation, and stereo point-cloud reconstruction.
